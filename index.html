About the Book

Wallace Barcloth died at thirty-five. Then he opened his eyes.

He's a newborn now, deep underground at the Knewsom Institute, in a body grown from what was left of his old one, wired to something the scientists call the singularity. Nobody has asked him what he wants.

All he wants is to find his wife.

But the thing on the other end of the Institute's experiment has been listening. When it finally acts, the world outside will start asking questions: a detective with a missing brother, a stranger far older than he looks, and something on the other side of a very old war that has just noticed Wal exists.

A Series

The Karkum Sequence

Some things are buried for a reason.

For thousands of years, a secret has lain hidden in the desert, half-remembered in legend and watched for by those who never forgot it. Its discovery sets in motion an end older than history, one that will reach every corner of the world.

There will be nowhere to hide. And nothing will be undone without cost.

About the Book

Archaeologist Richard Torrence knows the legend of Karkum: an ancient temple lost to the sands, and something on its wall that no one who saw it would ever describe. Invited to a dig in a desert nation barely free of its dictator, he finds the legend starting to feel less like folklore.

When the government shuts down the dig and ejects him from the country, Richard wants only to put it behind him. But someone isn't finished with him, and the people who might know what they want have secrets of their own.

About the Book

David Page is just a grad student when the world unravels in a single morning. While everyone around him writes it off as a publicity stunt, he does the one smart thing. He gets in his car and drives.

He makes it as far as a small farming town on the Colorado plains, where a few shaken survivors have taken shelter together, hoping for answers. At the edge of town, in the lot of an abandoned gas station, something sits silent, waiting.

Soon they'll learn the quiet won't last.
